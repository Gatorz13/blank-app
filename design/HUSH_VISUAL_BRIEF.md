# Hush Visual Brief — Product Truth

Hush is not trying to show fewer payments.

Hush is trying to show the **same payment volume** without creating a public relationship graph.

Public rails leak:
- balances
- counterparties
- timing
- dependencies

Hush accumulates:
- notes
- commitments
- nullifiers
- selective disclosures (scoped receipts)

Core homepage claim:
**Same payment activity in. Different visibility surface out.**
